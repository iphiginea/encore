Settings

Backup & Sync

Export your rankings as a file, then import it in another browser or on another device to bring everything over. Importing replaces whatever's currently on this device.

Encore also auto-exports a backup every 25 duels or 2 weeks, whichever comes first. Tip: set Safari's downloads to iCloud Drive (Settings → Safari → Downloads → iCloud Drive) so these land somewhere that syncs automatically.

Setlist Songs

Powers "Most Seen Songs Live" in Stats. New shows pick this up automatically — this fills it in for shows you logged before this existed. (Manually-added shows have no setlist to fetch.)

Venue Sizes (optional)

No free service provides venue capacity, so tag it yourself, once per venue — it then applies to every show there automatically. Unlocks the room-size Insights below.
